# TekDocs 0.2.8 retained publication artifact evidence

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: **complete and locally certified.** No push, tag, deployment, Wiki publication, or other external change was performed.

## Delivered scope

- Added signed `tekdocs-static-publication/v2` manifests and append-only `DocumentPublicationArtifact` records. Every new publication retains one generated PDF plus independent copies of every referenced managed attachment under opaque storage names.
- Added artifact size/checksum verification on download, stable universal entity identifiers in signed descriptors, complete-set validation before commit, forced RLS, scope/source guards, private forced downloads, and cleanup of partially written files after any failed publication.
- Added deterministic server-owned PDF rendering with fixed metadata, publication identity, reason/audience context, page numbering, headings, lists, code, and readable tables. Authored HTML/CSS and remote resources are excluded.
- Added required publication reason, `msp_internal`/`client_visible` intent, permanent/review-on-date retention, derived `review_due` state, and explicit no-purge behavior.
- Added append-only corrections and one-successor supersession. Current, superseded, and review-due states are derived without updating retained evidence; existing signed `v1` publications remain verifiable.
- Added Documentation-page metadata collection, confirmation, lifecycle labels, correction actions, PDF and retained attachment downloads, plus corresponding API/OpenAPI contracts and browser coverage.

## Known boundaries

- `client_visible` records intended audience only. It does not grant portal access or bypass `documents.view`; controlled client publication remains in `0.4.x`.
- Review-on-date is a reminder state, not a deletion instruction. TekDocs intentionally exposes no publication/artifact purge behavior.
- The retained Ed25519 signature proves deployment-key possession, not a qualified legal signature or external trusted timestamp.
- Existing `v1` publications are not retroactively given PDF/attachment artifacts. Their original signed snapshot remains readable and verifiable.
- The signing key remains environment-supplied until the `0.3.2` file-injection and operator secret-manager slice.
