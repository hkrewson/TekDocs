# TekDocs 0.5.9 portal and notification stabilization

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: **applicable local slice complete.** No push, tag, published image, deployment, hosted workflow, or external security review was performed.

## Delivered boundary

- Portal documents, notification inboxes, and delivery administration now expose bounded signed seek pages instead of silently stopping at a fixed history window.
- Cursors are short-lived and bound to the exact account and access surface; delivery cursors also bind their state filter.
- Notification display authorization batches subject and reference checks without weakening read-time permission enforcement.
- The portal and notification interfaces retain loaded results while fetching older pages and improve dialog focus, Escape behavior, busy state, and recovery behavior.
- A dedicated stabilization fixture covers 125 client-visible publications and 250 notification records under PostgreSQL query and latency budgets.

## Compatibility and limitations

This release has no schema migration and remains compatible with the `0.5.7` database. Existing histories become pageable without rewriting rows. Cursors may expire or become invalid after account/surface/filter changes and clients must restart from the newest page. The earlier `0.5.8` reminder/calendar slice was skipped, not implicitly delivered; that work is now `0.5.10`.
