# TekDocs 0.7.11

`0.7.11` adds safe asynchronous domain monitoring.

## Delivered

- Manual and hourly scheduled monitoring jobs with bounded queue/lease behavior.
- RDAP and DNS-over-HTTPS collection through the approved pinned-HTTPS egress boundary.
- Retained source, digest, expiration, registrar, DNS answer/count, and DNSSEC evidence without raw provider payloads.
- Append-only automated reconciliation that preserves entered registration truth.
- Exact-Workspace expiration, expiration-change, DNS-change, and collection-failure alerts.
- A Domains monitoring detail surface with queue state, source provenance, review result, notifications, and recent run history.
- PostgreSQL scope, immutability, and forced-RLS protection plus permission/IDOR, egress-abuse, migration, frontend, and generated-client coverage.

## Boundary

DNS evidence comes from the configured recursive DNS-over-HTTPS service. TekDocs records that source and does not claim a direct authoritative nameserver connection. Alerts are retained and visible in the Domains surface; general inbox/SMTP projection remains a separate recipient-routing extension so client boundaries cannot be bypassed.
