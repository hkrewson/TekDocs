# TekDocs 0.9.0 database-first baseline

Date: 2026-10-03

Status: **local baseline complete.** This commit freezes the database-first line before the version 1 managed-repository transition. No push, tag, image publication, deployment, hosted workflow, pilot, or independent security assessment is claimed.

## Delivered boundary

- Privileged void-before-delivery retains the issued invoice, number, signature, and artifact while blocking later delivery and client access.
- Separately numbered immutable credit notes copy exact frozen values, produce their own signed artifacts, link to the source invoice, and cannot cumulatively exceed its total.
- Recurring invoice enrollment, claims, review, versioned future terms, stop, withdrawal, and clean recovery are retained at the closeout boundary.
- The corrected Networks surface has an exact organization-scoped legacy inventory and safe VLAN, rack, IP-to-asset, and MAC-to-asset projections. Compatibility rows remain retained under ADR 0104.
- Remaining storage-dependent Documentation work and final candidate acceptance have explicit owners in `0.9.1` through `0.9.8`.

## Authority and safety

Issued financial content remains immutable at the database boundary. Corrections are separate append-only actions and require the established invoice permissions, MFA, recent reauthentication, exact tenant scope, and exact organization scope. Client portal reads exclude drafts, voided invoices, sibling organizations, and internal reconciliation details.

The Networks cleanup command previews exact counts before applying only lossless projections. It establishes tenant and organization RLS scope and never deletes legacy rows or clears historical relationships. Destructive cleanup requires a later deprecation and an operator-approved, recoverable migration.
