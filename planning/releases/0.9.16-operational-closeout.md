## Production and remaining scope

Production remains on 0.9.15. No production-host inspection, deployment or live
document migration was performed for this release. An explicitly authorized update can use
`scripts/update-production.sh --release v0.9.16` with the existing backup controls;
the receipt is already delivered by CI and needs no manual preparation.

Wiki changes remain local drafts under the separate publication contract.
Broader managed-file/template/key/reference/client-delivery parity and final
1.0 security, pilot and recovery acceptance remain open in the roadmap. This is
the bounded internal/permanent publication checkpoint described in the release
notes, not completion of 1.0.
