# TekDocs 0.3.5 client hardware lifecycle evidence

Date: 2026-08-10

Status: **implementation complete and locally certified.** No push, tag, image publication, deployment, Wiki publication, or other external change was performed.

## Delivered scope

- One current-state hardware profile for every client asset created from a hardware catalog model; software assets fail closed at all hardware lifecycle routes.
- Client-scoped, normalized serial numbers and asset tags; bounded acquisition and warranty metadata; and explicit in-stock, in-service, repair, retired, and disposed states.
- Current custody assignment to an active person association, structured site, and optional location from the exact client, with reassignment and unassignment support.
- Terminal disposal with date, method, reason, and automatic current-assignment clearing.
- An append-only typed lifecycle ledger for creation, material detail changes, state changes, assignment, unassignment, and disposal. Current state and history update in one locked transaction.
- Central `assets.view` and MFA-protected `assets.edit` decisions, CSRF enforcement, empty audit metadata, exact organization scoping, PostgreSQL relationship guards, forced RLS, immutable event/delete triggers, and sibling-client/cross-tenant negative coverage.
- A restrained hardware lifecycle panel within client Assets for identity, acquisition, warranty, assignment, disposal, and history, including responsive, keyboard, loading, empty, denial, and error behavior.
- A non-mocked browser journey that edits and assigns a real hardware asset through React and Django, then verifies exact current custody and ordered lifecycle events directly in PostgreSQL.

## Deferred boundaries

- Software installations, licenses, seats, renewals, and relationships remain `0.3.6`; costs and contracts remain `0.3.7`.
- Generic asset relationships, attachment-provider hardening, malware quarantine, and bulk operations are rescheduled to `0.3.9`; CSV import/export is rescheduled to `0.3.10`.
- Lifecycle history is deliberately value-minimized: acquisition/warranty references and disposal reasons remain in current state but are excluded from history events and audit metadata.
- Disposal is terminal through ordinary application and database-backed workflows. Any future correction mechanism requires a separate reviewed design and must append evidence rather than rewrite it.
- Hosted CI, CodeQL, DAST, external review, tags, containers, attestations, Wiki publication, and deployment require separate authorization and are not inferred from local evidence.

## Risk disposition

- `TD-RISK-023` is **mitigated with remaining owner**. Locked transactional mutations, exact client-scope validation, terminal disposal, value-minimized immutable events, PostgreSQL guards, forced RLS, and negative IDOR coverage prevent the identified current-state/history drift and foreign-custody paths. Concurrency at scale, archive semantics, full inventory restore, and larger-dataset performance remain owned by `0.3.11`.
