# TekDocs 0.4.1 network placement evidence

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: **implementation and applicable local evidence complete.** No push, tag, image publication, deployment, or other external change was performed.

## Delivered scope

- Stable exact-Workspace rack and network-device Entities for the one-MSP installation and client workspaces, with non-aggregating MSP routes.
- Structured site/location placement, optional hardware-asset provenance links, rack capacity, contiguous rack-unit placement, and searchable responsive management surfaces.
- Typed device relationships and backlinks through the existing permission-filtered EntityLink service, including symmetric `connected_to` links.
- Central `networks.view` and MFA-backed `networks.edit` enforcement, separate asset-field projection, value-safe audits, forced PostgreSQL RLS, and route-inventory coverage.
- Application row locks plus PostgreSQL advisory locks and guards for ownership edges, placement bounds, overlapping concurrent reservations, and contradictory rack moves or capacity changes.

## Risk disposition

- `TD-RISK-031` is **mitigated with remaining owners**: exact ownership, direct-write guards, concurrent overlap rejection, permission filtering, and non-aggregate routes are blocking now. Bulk/recovery/scale work remains `0.4.8`, and the complete network subsystem is certified at `0.5.0`.
- Existing `TD-RISK-001`, `TD-RISK-002`, `TD-RISK-003`, `TD-RISK-007`, `TD-RISK-017`, and `TD-RISK-018` controls were extended with route inventory, allow/deny and IDOR composition, RLS classification, a real browser journey, bounded pages, and migration-cycle evidence.

## Known boundaries

- This slice does not implement VLANs, VRFs, subnets, IP/MAC/interface records, circuits, wireless networks, diagrams, NetBox reconciliation, bulk transfer, recycle-bin recovery, or final scale certification. Those remain explicitly assigned to later `0.4.x` slices.
- Network devices reference hardware assets rather than copying catalog, model revision, serial, lifecycle, document provenance, or cost state. A user without `assets.view` receives neither linked asset identifiers nor names.
- `connected_to` is symmetric. Its storage direction is canonicalization detail; both devices receive the same permission-filtered relationship meaning.
- “Complete” records local repository evidence for this bounded slice. It is not third-party certification, a penetration test, or a hosted multi-MSP claim.
