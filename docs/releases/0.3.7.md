# TekDocs 0.3.7 commercial contracts and costs evidence

## Delivered

- Client Services now provides addressable commercial agreements related to eligible vendor, manufacturer, or partner organizations, including lifecycle, term, renewal, auto-renew, and notice state.
- Contract cost lines use fixed-precision amounts and quantities, normalized three-letter currency codes, bounded billing intervals, terms, and references.
- The established central sensitive-field policy now protects real financial records. Denied responses omit the complete `costs` member; contract search and result counts never inspect financial values.
- Contract operations use `assets.view` and MFA/CSRF-protected `assets.edit`; cost changes additionally require exact-scope `costs.view`. Scoped managers, PostgreSQL relationship guards, forced RLS, permission inventory, and IDOR tests cover every route.
- The organization Services page replaces its milestone placeholder with searchable contract, provider, renewal, archive, cost-entry/edit/archive, denied-cost, loading, empty, error, keyboard, responsive, and accessibility behavior.

## Boundary

This release does not provide accounting, invoices, taxes, currency conversion, totals/reporting, asset/license cost allocation, contract files, notification scheduling, or service dependency graphs. Those capabilities must define their own non-disclosure and authorization rules before they can consume financial values.

## Verification

- `make release-gate` passed against the production-shaped Docker stack.
- The current backend suite passed all 873 collected tests; coverage remained above the blocking 80% threshold. The commercial-focused PostgreSQL suite also passed after the final active-only mutation projection and database-forgery regressions were added.
- The frontend passed 166 tests across 41 files, lint, TypeScript, accessibility assertions, the production build, and shell/editor bundle budgets.
- Playwright passed 96 tests across Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit. The separate live Chromium journey created the contract and protected cost through the browser, then verified their exact Django/PostgreSQL records.
- Pip-audit, npm audit, Bandit, Gitleaks, and High/Critical Trivy image scans reported no findings.
- Fresh install, production secret-file injection, `0.1.3` database upgrade, `0.2.8` documentation upgrade, and database/media backup-and-restore rehearsals passed at `0.3.7`.
