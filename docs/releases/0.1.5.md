# TekDocs 0.1.5 People directory evidence

Date: 2026-08-08

Status: verified locally; hosted GitHub workflows remain pending publication and a pull request.

## Delivered scope

- A tenant-wide `Person` identity is separate from MSP- or organization-scoped employment/contact associations. Full and preferred name, phone, and email describe the identity; relationship type, role, responsibility, location label, and office describe the association.
- MSP and organization URLs expose list, create, detail, update, and archive contracts that resolve scope on the server. Installation-owner plus MFA remains the temporary policy boundary.
- PostgreSQL triggers reject a person anchored to the wrong tenant or organization scope and reject associations whose person or organization belongs to another tenant.
- The directory provides bounded server-side all-field search, allowlisted field filtering and sorting, pagination, accessible table semantics, responsive behavior, and locally retained non-sensitive visible-column preferences.
- Loading, empty, failure, denied-change, archive-confirmation, and stale-workspace states are explicit. A newly selected workspace cannot display the prior workspace's cached people while its request is pending.
- Create, update, and archive operations emit value-free audit events. List responses and browser preferences contain only fields authorized by the scoped API.
- ADR 0008 fixes the future authorization boundary: job titles never grant access; client assignments attach to authenticated users, support all-authorized-staff or assigned-staff-only modes, and remain subordinate to scoped RBAC permissions.

## Assigned risk disposition

- `TD-RISK-001` — **still open by design.** The People model prevents job titles from becoming permissions and records the client-assignment contract, but central policy, organization access modes, custom roles, and scoped user assignments remain `0.1.9`–`0.1.10`.
- `TD-RISK-002` — **still open.** Person integrity triggers and scoped managers are active; runtime-role RLS remains `0.1.12`.
- `TD-RISK-003` — **resolved for the People workflow; recurring obligation remains.** Browser creation and reload are verified through the proxy, Django, migrations, and PostgreSQL, followed by an independent database assertion.

## Known boundaries

- People administration is owner-plus-MFA only until RBAC ships. Client access assignment and client-user editing are not implied by this release.
- Creating a record creates a new identity and one association. Attaching an existing person is deferred until the typed-link/search foundation can prevent accidental identity merging.
- Identity-level phone, email, and name changes will require reuse-impact handling before ordinary scoped editors can modify a person shared across associations.
- `0.1.6` will add structured sites/locations while preserving free-text location and office labels for imports and unmapped rooms, desks, and remote work.
- PostgreSQL RLS, database-enforced audit immutability, secret-file enforcement, reproducible supply-chain inputs, backups, hosted automation evidence, and the editor bundle warning remain tracked in the engineering risk register.
