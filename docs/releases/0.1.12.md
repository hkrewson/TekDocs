# TekDocs 0.1.12 collection authorization and visibility evidence

Date: 2026-08-08

Status: verified locally; hosted GitHub workflows remain pending publication and a pull request.

## Delivered scope

- Tenant-owned access collections group exact active organizations for authorization. Collections have stable UUID identity, normalized unique names, descriptions, explicit membership replacement, archival, and no document-folder or general entity-grouping semantics.
- Collection-scoped custom roles grant permissions only inside active member organizations. They remain additive and cannot bypass tenant isolation or the independent `assigned_only` organization-access edge. Membership changes affect authorization immediately without rewriting role assignments.
- Owner-plus-MFA and CSRF-protected APIs manage access collections and their organization memberships. The Access Control interface adds collection creation, editing, archival, collection-role definition, and assignment workflows with impact confirmation and non-disclosing errors.
- Every entity now has a database-constrained visibility classification. `msp_private` is the default and a hard policy constraint; only explicit `client_visible` records in the exact organization can enter a future client-portal projection.
- `costs.view` is now a delegable permission enforced through a centralized field-projection seam. Denied cost fields are omitted, not returned as null or redacted placeholders, and granting cost access grants no other sensitive field.
- Workspace capabilities now include exact-organization and collection custom-role grants while preserving organization reachability as a separate prerequisite.
- PostgreSQL constraints and triggers enforce collection, membership, assignment, role-scope, visibility, active-target, creator, uniqueness, and immutable-identity invariants when application services are bypassed. A corrective refresh migration ensures already-migrated development and upgrade databases receive the final guard function.
- OpenAPI, architecture, information architecture, threat model, security guidance, engineering risks, ADR 0015, and the roadmap agree with the implemented boundary.

## Known boundaries

- Access collections are authorization groupings of organizations. They are not document collections, folders, arbitrary entity groups, or client-portal audiences.
- Custom roles remain additive grants. Tenant isolation, `assigned_only` reachability, MSP-private visibility, archive state, and field policy are hard constraints that take precedence.
- `client_visible` is a classification and projection contract, not authorization by itself. Client identities and portal workflows arrive in their scheduled slices.
- The cost-field seam is ready for asset and license projections; full cost-bearing domain records remain later roadmap work.
- Assignment removal and collection membership replacement are intentional configuration mutations recorded with value-free audit events. Collection archival preserves history and immediately disables its grants.
