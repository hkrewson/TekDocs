## Decision 1: inline-style CSP allowance

Finding: `TD-DAST-INLINE-STYLE`, medium; owner Maintainer / #38.

The candidate permits inline styles for existing layouts and generated diagrams.
This weakens protection against style injection. Same-origin script restrictions,
Markdown sanitization and diagram handling remain separate controls.

Accepted disposition: accept for **f52b210 only**, with review before the next
runtime candidate or **November 12, 2026**, whichever is earlier. Track narrower
style hashes/nonces or a compatible alternative in #38.

Alternative: remediate the style policy and verify compatibility before release,
then freeze and validate a new candidate.

Maintainer decision: **accepted for f52b210 only**, with review before the next
runtime candidate or November 12, 2026, whichever is earlier.

## Decision 2: absent Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy

Finding: `TD-DAST-COEP`, low; owner Maintainer / #38.

The candidate does not provide cross-origin isolation through COEP. Adding this
header requires compatibility work for OIDC flows, PDF workers, diagrams, fonts
and maintained browsers. Existing CSP and frame restrictions do not establish
COEP compatibility.

Accepted disposition: accept for **f52b210 only**, with review before the next
runtime candidate or **November 12, 2026**, whichever is earlier.

Alternative: implement COEP and verify compatibility before release, then freeze
and validate a new candidate.

Maintainer decision: **accepted for f52b210 only**, with review before the next
runtime candidate or November 12, 2026, whichever is earlier.
