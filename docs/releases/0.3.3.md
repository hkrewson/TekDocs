# TekDocs 0.3.3 supplier catalog evidence

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: **implementation complete and locally certified.** No push, tag, image publication, deployment, Wiki publication, or other external change was performed.

## Delivered scope

- Supplier-owned, universally addressable hardware/software product families and concrete models restricted to active Vendor or Manufacturer workspaces.
- Stable specification-set definitions with immutable sequential Draft 2020-12 schema versions and server-calculated SHA-256 checksums.
- Immutable sequential model revisions pinned to exact specification versions, with server-side validation, parent linkage, content checksums, readable history, and stale-write `409` protection.
- Central `assets.view`/`assets.edit` authorization, MFA/CSRF mutation requirements, exact supplier scoping, scoped managers, PostgreSQL relationship guards, forced RLS, and append-only database triggers.
- Supplier reclassification protection: a catalog owner must retain a Vendor or Manufacturer classification, while an atomic switch between those classifications remains supported.
- A restrained supplier Products workspace with search, hardware/software filters, structured specification authoring, product/model creation, revision editing/history, and loading, empty, error, responsive, keyboard, and accessibility states.
- A non-mocked browser journey that creates a supplier, specification set, product, model, and second revision through React, Django, and PostgreSQL, then verifies persisted immutable relationships directly.

## Deferred boundaries

- Client-owned asset instantiation, retained supplier provenance, product documentation projection, and relationship-derived client vendor lists remain `0.3.4`.
- Hardware serials, assignment, warranty, acquisition/disposal, and lifecycle history remain `0.3.5`.
- Product/model images, arbitrary document-authored schema logic, remote schema references, authored regular expressions, scripts, and executable extensions are not accepted.
- Hosted CI, CodeQL, DAST, external review, tags, containers, attestations, Wiki publication, and deployment require separate authorization and are not inferred from local evidence.
