are unchanged. No application/API/schema/permission/dependency/version change is
made. Published 0.9.17 images, tags and updater receipt retain their original
identity; this is subsequent CI tooling, not a replacement release or deployment.

Only failed rehearsals collect the new diagnostics, before existing cleanup:

- Worker, scheduler and queue running/restarting/OOM booleans and exit code.
  Missing/unreadable state is reported as unavailable.
