# TekDocs 0.3.4 client asset provenance evidence

Date: 2026-08-10

Status: **implementation complete and locally certified.** No push, tag, image publication, deployment, Wiki publication, or other external change was performed.

## Delivered scope

- Supplier product and optional model associations to one exact, verified, immutable, `client_visible` STATIC publication owned by that supplier.
- Client-owned asset entities created from active supplier models with the exact supplier, product, model, model revision, specification-definition version, validated values, and a server-calculated provenance checksum retained at creation.
- Append-only asset-document provenance capturing every applicable product-level and model-level association, exact publication identity, and exact content digest.
- Permission-aware retained STATIC document viewing and artifact download from the client asset without granting general access to the supplier workspace.
- A derived client Vendors page that groups active assets by retained supplier identity without copying organizations, matching mutable labels, or creating implicit entity relationships.
- Central `documents.view`, `assets.view`, and `assets.edit` decisions; MFA/CSRF mutation protection; exact organization scoping; PostgreSQL relationship guards; forced RLS; append-only database triggers; and sibling-client, sibling-supplier, and cross-tenant negative coverage.
- Lazy-loaded supplier Products, client Assets, and client Vendors interfaces with loading, empty, error, denial, responsive, keyboard, and accessibility behavior.
- A non-mocked browser journey that publishes a supplier guide, associates it with a model, creates a client asset, opens its retained guide, verifies the derived vendor, and confirms the immutable relationships directly in PostgreSQL.

## Deferred boundaries

- Hardware serials, assignment, acquisition/disposal, warranty, and mutable lifecycle history remain `0.3.5`.
- Software installations, licenses, seats, and renewals remain `0.3.6`; costs and contracts remain `0.3.7`.
- Existing assets deliberately retain their original revision, specifications, publication identities, and digests. Later supplier revisions, publication corrections, or association changes apply only to future assets.
- Display labels are resolved from the retained UUID identities for current presentation; provenance and checksum construction never derive identity from a label.
- Hosted CI, CodeQL, DAST, external review, tags, containers, attestations, Wiki publication, and deployment require separate authorization and are not inferred from local evidence.

## Risk disposition

- `TD-RISK-022` is **mitigated with remaining owner**. Exact UUID/revision/schema/publication identities, validated values, signed-publication verification, checksums, database guards, immutable document-provenance rows, and narrow read-only client catalog projection prevent label-based or browser-forged provenance. Scale, archive, upgrade, and restore certification continues through `0.3.11`.
