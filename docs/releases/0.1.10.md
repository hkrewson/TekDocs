# TekDocs 0.1.10 explicit client staff assignment evidence

Date: 2026-08-08

Status: verified locally; hosted GitHub workflows remain pending publication and a pull request.

## Delivered scope

- Tenant-owned organization staff assignments connect one tenant membership to one organization without using Person records, job titles, or contact metadata as authorization inputs.
- The central policy service composes tenant-role permission with organization access mode and explicit assignment. Assignment never grants a permission, satisfies MFA, or changes a role.
- The installation owner retains break-glass access. Assignment administration itself remains owner-plus-MFA and CSRF protected.
- Owner APIs list, add, and remove assignments through scoped organization and member identifiers. Duplicate adds and missing-edge removals are safe idempotent retries, while successful changes produce value-free audit events.
- PostgreSQL rejects cross-tenant assignment edges and changes to assignment identity or ownership.
- Organization lists, workspace search and resolution, scoped domain APIs, Entity search, and relationship traversal use the same policy-filtered organization set.
- The Access Control interface shows assigned staff per organization, confirms adds/removals, and states that the existing MSP role continues to determine capability.
- The Domains roadmap now separates registered-domain inventory, subdomain/DNS hierarchy, renewal schedules, safe RDAP/DNS collection, and TLS endpoint validation into six bounded `0.7.8`–`0.7.13` slices.

## Assigned risk disposition

- `TD-RISK-001` — **partially mitigated.** Explicit per-client MSP staff assignment now composes with built-in tenant roles. Custom roles, scoped role grants, collection scope, MSP-private constraints, and sensitive-field policy remain `0.1.11`–`0.1.12`.
- `TD-RISK-002` — **still open.** PostgreSQL enforces assignment integrity, but active non-owner runtime-role RLS remains `0.1.14` and certification remains `0.2.0`.
- `TD-RISK-003` — **resolved for this workflow; recurring obligation remains.** The browser, proxy, Django policy service, invitation flow, assignment API, and PostgreSQL row were exercised together.

## Known boundaries

- These are MSP staff reachability assignments, not client-portal identities or organization-scoped roles.
- Client Administrator and Client User remain cataloged but unassignable until the scoped-role model lands.
- Custom roles, collection-scoped assignment, cost/secret field policy, RLS, audit immutability, and the complete permission/IDOR matrix remain scheduled and release-blocking at their owning milestones.
- Domain inventory and monitoring are intentionally planned rather than implemented here. External RDAP, DNS, and TLS probes must wait for the approved egress/SSRF boundary.
